Questions

Pricing questions

Who sets the hourly rate?

Each tutor sets their own rate, based on subject, level and experience. You see it on every profile before you book.

Are there any extra fees?

No. The price you see on the tutor's profile is what you pay per class.

How does the sibling discount work?

From the second child in the same family, every class is 10% cheaper.

What happens if a class is missed?

If the tutor misses a paid class, you get a refund or a free replacement class. Your money is only released to the tutor after the class.
