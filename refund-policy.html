Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Draft: this text is a starting point and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

We want every family to feel safe paying in advance. Your payment is held by us and is released to the tutor only after the class has taken place.

1. Free demo class

The first demo with each tutor is free, so there is nothing to refund.

2. If the tutor misses a class

If the tutor does not join a paid class, or cancels less than 12 hours before it, you can choose a full refund for that class or a free replacement class.

3. If you cancel

  • Cancel 12 hours or more before the class: full refund or move the class to another time.
  • Cancel less than 12 hours before, or do not join: the class is counted as completed.

4. Technical problems

If a class cannot happen because of a problem on our side, you get a free replacement class or a refund.

5. Monthly packages

  • Unused classes move to the next month.
  • You can stop a package at any time and get a refund for classes not yet taken, at the per-class package price.
  • If you switch tutor, your unused classes move to the new tutor.

6. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method, or as account credit if you prefer. They are usually processed within 5–7 working days.

7. How to ask for a refund

Use the "Report a problem" button on the class in your dashboard, or contact us within 7 days of the class.
