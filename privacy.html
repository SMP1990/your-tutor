Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Draft: this text is a starting point and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

This policy explains what personal information your tutor collects, why, and how we protect it. We collect as little as we need, especially about children.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, email and password (stored encrypted).
  • Student details: first name, grade and subjects. We do not ask for a child's photo or ID.
  • Tutor details: qualifications, ID and documents for checks, profile photo and video, and payout details.
  • Class details: bookings, attendance, class notes, reviews and, only with consent, recordings.
  • Payments: amounts and status. Card details are entered on the payment provider's page and never reach our servers.

2. Why we use it

  • To run your account, book classes and process payments and refunds.
  • To send class reminders on WhatsApp or email, only if you agreed to receive them.
  • To keep the platform safe and to answer support requests.

3. Who can see it

  • Tutors see only what they need for their own classes.
  • Parents see their own children's classes, attendance and notes.
  • Service providers we use (payments, video classes, messaging, hosting) process data only to provide their service.
  • We never sell personal information.

4. Class recordings

Classes are recorded only with consent, and classes of children only with a parent's consent. Recordings can be viewed only by the family, the tutor and, for safety reviews, our support team.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as the law requires afterwards. You can ask us to delete your account.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, and you can stop WhatsApp or email messages at any time.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted passwords and strict access rules. Uploaded documents are stored privately.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us.
