Parents & students

How do I find the right tutor?

Use Find a Tutor to filter by subject, grade, price and rating. Open a profile to see qualifications, reviews and availability, then book a free demo.

Is the demo class really free?

Yes. The first 30-minute demo with each tutor is free and needs no payment details.

What do we need for an online class?

A phone, tablet or laptop with a camera, and a stable internet connection. Earphones help.

Can parents watch the class?

Yes. Parents can sit in, and they see attendance and class notes in their dashboard.

Are classes recorded?

Only if you want. Classes for children are recorded only with a parent's consent, and only the family and tutor can view them.

Can we switch tutor?

Yes, at any time. Unused paid classes move with you to the new tutor.

Payments & refunds

How much does a class cost?

Each tutor sets their own hourly rate, usually PKR 1,000 to 3,000. Monthly packages save 10%.

How can I pay?

JazzCash, Easypaisa, debit or credit card, or bank transfer.

When does the tutor get my money?

We hold your payment and release it to the tutor only after the class has taken place.

What if a class does not happen?

If the tutor misses a paid class, you get a refund or a free replacement class. See the Refund Policy for details.

Tutors

How do I become a tutor?

Apply online, pass a short subject test and a live interview. Once approved, your profile goes live.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Applying and creating your profile is free. A platform fee is taken from each paid class.

How do I get paid?

Your earning is added to your balance after each completed class. Withdraw it to your bank account, JazzCash or Easypaisa.
