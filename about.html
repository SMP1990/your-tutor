About us

Good teaching, wherever you live

We connect students across Pakistan with verified tutors for live one-to-one online classes, from Grade 1 to A Levels and entry tests.

Why we started

Finding a good tutor in Pakistan usually depends on who you know. Many families pay for home tuition without knowing if the tutor is qualified, and students in smaller cities often have no good options at all.

We built your tutor to change that. Parents can compare tutors, read real reviews and try a free demo class before paying anything. Students learn from home, and tutors reach students far beyond their own city.

Our mission

Make trusted, affordable tutoring available to every student in Pakistan, with clear prices and progress parents can follow.
