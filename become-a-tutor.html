Requirements

What you need

  • A degree in your subject, or a university student for Grade 1–8 subjects
  • Your CNIC (for the identity check)
  • Laptop or smartphone with a working camera and microphone
  • Stable internet connection
  • Clear Urdu and/or English
  • Pass a short subject test and a live interview

How you get paid

Students pay before the class. When the class is completed, your earning is added to your balance. Withdraw it from your tutor dashboard to:

  • Bank account
  • JazzCash
  • Easypaisa
How to apply

Four steps to your first student

  1. 1 Apply online Create a tutor account, add your subjects and upload your degree and ID.
  2. 2 Subject test and interview A short test in your subject and a live interview with our team.
  3. 3 Your profile goes live Set your rate and available times. Students can now find you and book demos.
  4. 4 Teach and get paid Teach from home. Your earnings are released after each completed class and you withdraw them from your dashboard.

Questions

Tutor questions

Do I have to pay to join?

No. Applying and creating your profile is free.

How much does the platform keep?

A small platform fee is taken from each paid class. The exact fee will be shown before you sign up.

How many hours must I teach?

There is no minimum. You choose your own times and can pause your profile when you are busy.

Do I teach the free demo for free?

Yes, the first demo with a new student is free. It is your chance to win a regular student.
