Online tutoring, step by step

From finding a tutor to the first class in a few days. Here is how it works for families and for tutors.

For parents & students

Four steps to the right tutor

  1. 1

    Find the right tutor

    Search by subject and grade. Compare profiles, qualifications, reviews and hourly rates side by side.

    • Every tutor is checked before they can teach
    • See availability before you book
  2. 2

    Take a free demo class

    Book a free 30-minute online class. Meet the tutor and see if the teaching style suits the student.

    • No payment needed for the demo
    • We confirm the time with you on WhatsApp
  3. 3

    Start regular classes

    Happy with the demo? Choose pay per session or a monthly package and pick fixed class times.

    • No long contract
    • Switch tutor at any time
  4. 4

    Follow the progress

    Parents see attendance, class notes and tutor feedback after every class in their dashboard.

    • Reminders before every class
    • Session notes after every class

Online classes

What a class looks like

Live video class

One-to-one video in the browser on phone or laptop. Nothing to install.

Screen share and whiteboard

Tutors explain on screen, solve numericals step by step and share files.

Class recordings

Classes can be recorded for revision. For children, only with parent consent.

Notes after class

The tutor writes what was covered and what to practise before the next class.

Payments

Simple, safe payments

Pay per session or monthly

Pay for single classes, or save with a monthly package. Prices are shown on every tutor profile.

Your money is held safely

We hold the payment and release it to the tutor only after the class has taken place.

Refund if a class does not happen

If a tutor misses a paid class, you get a refund or a free replacement class.

For tutors

Teach online and earn from home

  1. 1 Apply online Create a tutor account, add your subjects and upload your degree and ID.
  2. 2 Subject test and interview A short test in your subject and a live interview with our team.
  3. 3 Your profile goes live Set your rate and available times. Students can now find you and book demos.
  4. 4 Teach and get paid Teach from home. Your earnings are released after each completed class and you withdraw them from your dashboard.

Questions

Common questions

Is the demo class really free?

Yes. The first demo class with a tutor is free and needs no payment details.

What do we need for an online class?

A phone, tablet or laptop with a camera and a stable internet connection. Earphones help.

Can parents watch the class?

Yes. Parents can sit in, and they see attendance and class notes in their dashboard.

What if we do not like the tutor?

You can switch to another tutor at any time. Unused paid classes move with you.

Book a free demo class today

Meet up to three tutors before you decide. No payment needed for the demo.
